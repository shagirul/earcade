Each route is bipolar except tremolo. Pitch is in cents; filter depth is in octaves. Wavetable scan is updated at control rate.

Signal path: drive → chorus → delay → reverb → master compressor. Effects are shared across all voices.

Voice & performance

Up to 12 simultaneous notes. In mono mode, glide is applied between successive notes. A4 = 440 Hz.

Patch management

User presets are saved locally in this browser. Export JSON to keep a portable backup. This app does not require a network connection.
